Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

The Stoic Launcher application (hereinafter referred to as the "App") is built with user privacy as a top priority. This policy describes how the App handles your data.

1. Data Collection and Use

The App is designed to operate locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to any external servers.

The App requires access to the following information to function correctly:

  • List of installed applications: To allow you to select apps for your home screen.
  • Usage Statistics: To display screen time and usage statistics.
  • Date of Birth: If provided, it is used solely to calculate "days alive" for stoic reflection. This data is stored only locally on your phone.
  • Sleep Time: Used to calculate the countdown until sleep. Stored only locally.

2. Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. All settings and data remain on your device.

3. Permissions

The App may request specific Android system permissions (e.g., setting as default launcher, access to usage data). These permissions are used exclusively for the features described above.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We recommend checking this page periodically.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, you can contact me via the contact information provided in the Google Play Store.
